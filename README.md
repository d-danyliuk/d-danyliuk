# Hi, I'm Denys Danyliuk

Founder & CEO of [Corvamed](https://github.com/corvamed) ([corvamed.com](https://corvamed.com)), an AI service in development that will help patients find clinical trials they may qualify for, explain them in plain words, and help research teams reach interested, pre-checked participants.

Before Corvamed, I built NLP pipelines for patient eligibility analysis in clinical trials at Lindus Health and LLM-based tools for scientific literature and clinical trial documentation at Roche pRED. MSc AI/ML, Imperial College London.

## What I work on

- **Clinical NLP:** matching patient data with trial eligibility criteria. See how the AI in Corvamed is designed in [corvamed/responsible-ai](https://github.com/corvamed/responsible-ai).
- **Large language models** for medical and scientific text, with fact-checking and reliable answers.
- **Privacy-preserving machine learning** on sensitive health data. Read [how we plan to handle health data](https://github.com/corvamed/responsible-ai#data-and-consent).
